NIGERIA TENANT RIGHTS
LEGAL DOCUMENT STARTER PACK
For the Legal Aid AI Assistant project | Nationwide scope: 36 States + FCT Important note This is a nationwide document-collection and ingestion starter pack, not a single consolidated Nigerian tenancy statute. Tenancy and recovery-of-premises rules vary by state and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT), and some rules may depend on the type of premises, tenancy agreement, location, and later amendments. The AI must identify where the property is located and cite the exact law and section before giving jurisdiction-specific guidance. Do not treat this checklist as proof that every listed law is current or applicable.
1. Core source documents and research portals • Lagos State Tenancy Law 2011 (official Lagos State Ministry of Justice PDF) https://lagosstatemoj.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Tenancy-Law-2011.pdf A useful example of a state tenancy statute. Verify amendments, scope, and statutory exceptions before use. • Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (National Library of Nigeria repository) https://nigeriareposit.nln.gov.ng/items/399df25a-52d0-4972-9365-c618cd3ddf5f Nationwide constitutional background; not a substitute for state tenancy legislation. • Senate Committee on Constitution Review — Constitution text and structure https://sccr.gov.ng/?page_id=111 Reference for the Constitution and its chapters. • PLAC — Laws of Nigeria searchable compendium https://www.placng.org/lawsofnigeria/ Useful federal-law research portal. Its own FAQ warns the LFN 2004 compilation is not fully updated for all later federal enactments; check later Acts separately. • Federal Ministry of Justice — publications / Laws of the Federation https://justice.gov.ng/publications-of-the-department-of-law-reporting-and-publication/ Official federal publication channel and information about federal law publications. • Recovery of Premises Act, Cap. 544 (PDF copy hosted by Sabi Law) https://sabilaw.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/RECOVERY-OF-PREMISES-ACT-1945_watermark.pdf Research copy relevant to the FCT context; verify against an authoritative current source and applicable amendments. • FCT High Court judgment discussing the Recovery of Premises Act https://www.fcthighcourt.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/SULEIMAN-ABDULFATAI-VS.-EDWIN-APRA-3- ORS-TENANCY-RECOVERY-OF-PREMISES.pdf Illustrates how courts refer to the Act in a tenancy/recovery dispute; use judgments as interpretation, not as a replacement for the statute. • Kwara State Rent Control and Recovery of Residential Premises Law https://kwaralaws.ng/product/r2rent-control-and-recovery-of-residential-premises-law/ State-law example. Verify the latest amendments and official-gazette status. • Kwara State Landlord and Tenant Law https://kwaralaws.ng/product/l4landlord-and-tenant-law/ State-law example. Check scope and exclusions, including customary or Islamic-law tenancies where relevant. • Nigerian State Law Compendium — tenancy and recovery-of-premises topic https://nigerianstatelaws.aalawsng.com/topics/tenancy-recovery-of-premises A discovery/comparison aid only. The page indicates some jurisdictions are still under review; verify each result against primary legislation. • Legal Aid Act — PLAC searchable text https://www.placng.org/lawsofnigeria/view2.php?sn=232 Potential source for legal-aid eligibility and service context. Verify current amendments and scope.

3. Documents to collect for each jurisdiction • Current principal tenancy / landlord-and-tenant law, if enacted. • Current Recovery of Premises Act or Law and all amendments. • Rent-control or rent-regulation legislation, if applicable. • Applicable court rules and forms for possession/recovery proceedings. • Relevant published court judgments, especially on notice, eviction, rent arrears, self-help eviction, and jurisdiction. • Official legal-aid or citizens' advice information, including contact details and eligibility. • Official Gazette or other authoritative source proving the enactment and amendment status. • Where relevant, laws or rules affecting customary, Islamic, commercial, public, institutional, or other special tenancies.

5. Tenant-rights question categories for the AI • Eviction and recovery of possession; notice to quit; notice of intention to recover possession. • Rent increases, advance rent, rent arrears, rent receipts, and rent deposits. • Landlord entry, privacy, locks changed, utility disconnection, harassment, and alleged self-help eviction. • Repairs, habitability, service charges, maintenance duties, and damage to property. • Tenancy agreement clauses, oral tenancies, renewal, subletting, assignment, and fixed-term tenancies. • Refund of deposits or unused rent, property abandonment, and disputes after the tenancy ends. • Commercial versus residential premises; statutory exemptions and special categories. • Where to seek help: lawyer, legal-aid office, mediation service, police only where appropriate, and the competent court.
6. Required answer behaviour for the Legal Aid AI
1. Ask which state/FCT the property is in before giving jurisdiction-specific advice.
2. Ask whether the premises are residential or commercial and clarify the tenancy type when relevant.
3. Retrieve and cite the exact law title, section/subsection, and source link for legal claims.
4. Distinguish statutory law, contract terms, court interpretation, and general practical information.
5. Never assume Lagos law applies throughout Nigeria or that one notice period applies everywhere.
6. If a law's current status or amendment history is uncertain, say so and direct the user to a lawyer or official source.
7. Do not guarantee a legal outcome; flag urgent risks such as threatened physical removal or lockout and recommend prompt local legal help.
8. Log the source passages used so the answer can be audited and corrected.
7. Suggested starter test questions • My landlord changed the locks while my belongings are inside. What should I do? • How much notice must my landlord give before ending my tenancy in [STATE]? • Can my landlord demand two years' rent in advance in [STATE]? • My landlord has refused to repair a serious leak. What are my options? • Can a landlord increase rent during a fixed-term tenancy? • My tenancy agreement says the landlord can remove my things without going to court. Is that clause enforceable? • I paid rent in advance but had to leave early. Can I recover unused rent? • Which authority or legal-aid service can help with a tenancy dispute in [STATE/FCT]?
